## Research

### [Mind2Text — EEG decoding with LLMs](https://github.com/alikaz3mi/mind2text)
Symbolic tokenization of EEG signals + LoRA-fine-tuned LLMs for cognitive-state classification. **78.2% accuracy**, outperforming CNN and SVM baselines. An active research line I want to extend in a PhD.

### M.Sc. Thesis — Diffusion-tensor modeling of brain development
*Modeling of Human Brain Perisylvian Pathways Development with Diffusion Tensor Imaging.* Characterized microstructural development of neonatal language pathways; manuscript in preparation (Kazemi, Abrishami Moghaddam, Namiranian).
