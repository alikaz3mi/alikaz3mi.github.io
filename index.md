## Research

### Mind2Text — EEG decoding with LLMs

Symbolic tokenization of EEG signals with LoRA-fine-tuned LLMs for cognitive-state classification. **78.2% accuracy**, outperforming CNN and SVM baselines. An active research line I want to extend in a PhD. [Code on GitHub](https://github.com/alikaz3mi/mind2text).

### M.Sc. Thesis — Diffusion-tensor modeling of brain development

*Modeling of Human Brain Perisylvian Pathways Development with Diffusion Tensor Imaging.* Characterized microstructural development of neonatal language pathways; a paper from this work is in progress (Kazemi, Abrishami Moghaddam, Namiranian).
